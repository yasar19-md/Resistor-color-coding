How to Read Resistor Colour Codes

Four simple steps from bands to value.

Read from left to right

  1. Identify the bandsHold the resistor so the tolerance band, usually spaced farther away, is on the right.
  2. Read the significant digitsFor a 4-band resistor, the first two bands form the number. A 5-band resistor uses three.
  3. Apply the multiplierMultiply your significant digits by the value represented by the next band.
  4. Check toleranceThe final band tells you how far the real resistance may vary from the stated value.

The formula

Resistance = Significant Digits × Multiplier

Minimum = Resistance − Tolerance

Maximum = Resistance + Tolerance
